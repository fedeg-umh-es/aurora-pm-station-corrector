# Claim Boundaries — Aurora PM Station Corrector

**Registration date:** 2026-08-06  
**Phase:** A (registration, no results)

This document defines the interpretation space for any results generated by
this project. It must be read before drafting any conclusion, abstract, or
discussion section.

---

## 1. Permitted Interpretations

The following conclusions are permitted if supported by out-of-sample
evidence obtained under the registered protocol:

- Aurora forecasts contain **incremental predictive information** for PM10
  and/or PM2.5 at station scale, conditional on CAMS forecasts and local
  observational history available at emission time.
- The incremental content improves (or does not improve) detection,
  magnitude, duration, or timing of elevated-PM episodes at a specific
  horizon and station type.
- For a particular contrast (e.g. `local_plus_cams_plus_aurora` vs
  `local_plus_cams`), the difference in MAE or skill score is statistically
  distinguishable from zero at the significance level reported, after
  multiplicity correction.
- African dust regime conditions the magnitude of the performance difference
  (as a subgroup analysis, not a primary claim).

---

## 2. Prohibited Interpretations

### 2.1 Causal claims

- "Aurora has learned new physics not present in CAMS."
- "The improvement proceeds from [any specific mechanism]."
- "Aurora is independent of CAMS."

Aurora Air Pollution was fine-tuned using CAMS products. Any overlap in
signal between Aurora and CAMS is expected; no causal separation can be
drawn from regression increments.

### 2.2 Independence claims

- Treating Aurora and CAMS as independent inputs without documenting the
  training dependency is prohibited.
- Any discussion of complementary information must explicitly acknowledge
  that Aurora's training used CAMS-derived targets.

### 2.3 Novelty claims (prohibited)

The following are established methodology or prior art and must not be
presented as contributions of this project:

| Item | Reason |
|---|---|
| Rolling-origin / expanding-window validation | Standard temporal CV protocol |
| MOS, EMOS, bias correction | Decades-old post-processing family |
| LightGBM or gradient-boosting residual correction | Standard ML pipeline |
| Station-scale evaluation of NWP/AI models | Standard operational meteorology |
| Aurora raw smoothing of maxima | Expected behaviour of probabilistic AI models |
| Threshold-based episode detection | Standard air quality practice |
| Regional application in Comunitat Valenciana | Prior regional studies exist |
| Simple Aurora-raw vs Aurora-corrected comparison | Prior published comparisons exist |

### 2.4 Premature conclusions

- No conclusion may be written based on synthetic, dummy, or placeholder data.
- No conclusion may be written before Phase C out-of-sample predictions exist.
- Aggregate metrics across horizons must not be the sole basis for any claim.

---

## 3. Prohibited Methodological Choices

| Prohibition | Consequence if violated |
|---|---|
| Random or shuffled cross-validation | Nullifies temporal validity; results discarded |
| Preprocessing fitted on full series | Leakage; results discarded |
| Using observations after `available_time` | Leakage; results discarded |
| Pooling horizons in a single DM test | Statistical error; test invalid |
| Thresholds derived from test data | Leakage; event metrics invalid |

---

## 4. Conditional Acceptance Criteria

A result may be reported if and only if:

1. All 9 model variants produced out-of-sample predictions at the relevant
   horizon.
2. The leakage test passed (automated CI, non-zero exit on violation).
3. Provenance metadata is complete for the run.
4. Thresholds were estimated from train data only.
5. DM tests are reported per (pollutant, horizon, contrast), not pooled.
6. The Aurora–CAMS training dependency is acknowledged in the methods section.

---

## 5. Regime Analysis Scope

African dust or other atmospheric regimes may be used as subgroup conditioning
variables in post-hoc analysis. This is exploratory and must be reported as
such. Regime-conditional results must not be elevated to primary claims without
pre-registration of the regime definition and the associated hypothesis.

---

## 6. Version Control

Any modification to this document after Phase B data are available must be
committed with an explicit comment stating what changed and why. Retroactive
changes that weaken a falsification criterion are prohibited without external
review.
